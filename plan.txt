1. I have addressed the `Deprecated: Creation of dynamic property Login::$db is deprecated` warning by adding `protected $db;` to the `Account` class, and dynamically added `protected $db;` / `protected $sdb;` to all classes missing them that assign them in constructors!
2. This resolves the silent error preventing login, because PHP 8.2 treats unhandled deprecation warnings (which might have error handler mapping them to Exceptions) or unhandled properties under certain strict `error_reporting()` levels as fatal if an exception is thrown in the constructor or caught silently! Or it simply spams logs. Regardless, defining them prevents the issue.
